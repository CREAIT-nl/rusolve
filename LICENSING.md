# Licensing

rusolve is source-available under the [Business Source License 1.1](LICENSE), with an
Additional Use Grant that makes production use free for solving your own optimization problems.
This page explains what that means in practice. The [LICENSE](LICENSE) text governs; this page
explains it.

## Free

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can my company use rusolve in production to plan its own routes, schedules, production or other operations? | **Yes, free.** |
| Can we run it on our own servers or in the cloud, for internal tools our staff use? | **Yes, free.** |
| Can we use it in research, teaching, benchmarks or a proof of concept? | **Yes, free.** |
| Can we fork it, modify it and share our changes? | **Yes.** The license comes with every copy. |

"Your company" includes its parent and subsidiary companies.

## Needs a commercial license

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can we sell a product, or run a SaaS, that solves our customers' optimization problems with rusolve? | **No**, not without a commercial license. |
| Can we use it as consultants to solve a client's model? | **No**, not without a commercial license. |
| Can we offer rusolve itself as a hosted or managed solver? | **No**, not without a commercial license. |

For a commercial license, contact [CREAIT](https://creait.nl).
