# Code of conduct

rusolve follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, critique code rather than people,
and keep discussions on topic. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the repository's private
reporting channel (**Security → Report a vulnerability**; say that it is a conduct report).
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or ban
participants who break these rules.
